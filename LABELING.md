# Labeling rules

**Label 1 = scam/spam:** tries to get money, personal info, OTPs, or clicks.

Includes:
- fake prizes
- loan offers
- KYC / account-block threats
- job offers asking for fees
- unknown betting or lottery links

**Label 0 = normal:**
- personal chats
- genuine bank alerts
- delivery updates
- OTP messages you requested
- regular promotions from known brands

**Edge case:** promotional messages from real shops = 0, unless they ask for money or info.

**Language:** Telugu script, Tenglish (Telugu in English letters), or mixed.
